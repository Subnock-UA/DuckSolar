## b2.0.0

- Separate public line of all four programs, with distinct Android IDs, ports,
  Windows storage, shortcuts/autostart/mutex and equipment-free first startup.
- Blank identity/network defaults; removed private coordinates, local build paths,
  implicit old-profile discovery and bundled house-meter readings/seeds.
- Neutral energy settings, unset battery capacity and configurable signing paths.
- Preserved server forecastv4 actual data through client adapters, plus separate
  metadata-check and verified-download/install workflows.
- English and Ukrainian interface selection in every component, English by
  default, with independent saved preferences and presentation-only translation.
- Both languages of installation, configuration, user/developer documentation
  and separate AI maintenance instructions.
- Source and nested artifact privacy audits, isolated regressions and self-checks.

The user explicitly requested replacement of the existing b2 build without a
version increment. Product b2.0.0, APK code20000 and PC PE2.0.0.0 remain unchanged.
Install an existing b2.0.0 replacement manually; normal update guards are intact.
